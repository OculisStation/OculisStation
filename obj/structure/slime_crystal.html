slime_crystal
/obj/structure/slime_crystal

Base pylon, one subtype per slime color.

Vars

affected_mobsAssoc list of affected mobs, the key is the mob while the value of the map is the number of seconds spent inside of the zone.
colourThe slime color this pylon is made from, used for its name and tint.
effect_rangeHow far the pylon reaches, for the mob tracker and for anything that needs line of sight.
mob_trackerWatches the living mobs in range when tracks_mobs is set.
tracks_mobsDoes it watch the living mobs in range, and run on_mob_enter/effect/leave on them?
uses_processDoes it use process?

Procs

add_affected_mobStarts tracking a mob, and calls on_mob_enter on it.
on_mob_effectCalled every process tick on each tracked mob.
on_mob_enterCalled when a mob starts being tracked.
on_mob_leaveCalled when a mob stops being tracked, including when the pylon is destroyed.
remove_affected_mobStops tracking a mob, and calls on_mob_leave on it.
tint_to_slime_colorTints the pylon to its slime's color, or gives it the slime's visual effect.
