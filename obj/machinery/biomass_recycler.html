biomass_recycler
/obj/machinery/biomass_recycler

Turns creatures into biomass, and biomass into cubes and creatures.

Vars

baseline_speciesCritters you can print right away, no unlocks needed.
biomassBiomass available for printing cubes and creatures.
printable_itemsItems available from the machine's radial menu.
recycling_efficiencyMultiplier applied to biomass gained from recycled creatures.
